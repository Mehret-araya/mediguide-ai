## Review checks

- [ ] **Accuracy:** The definition as a *Plasmodium* infection and the description of the main mosquito-borne transmission route are accurate and not overstated.
- [ ] **Other transmission routes:** Less common routes are clearly described as less common and are supported by the cited references.
- [ ] **Symptoms:** Fever, chills, headache, muscle aches, and malaise are accurately described as common symptoms, not as a diagnosis.
- [ ] **Severe complications:** Anaemia, jaundice, kidney failure, enlarged spleen, and cerebral-malaria complications are accurately stated and framed as possible outcomes.
- [ ] **Ethiopian suitability:** The wording is understandable and appropriate for general education in Ethiopia; European surveillance and travel context is not presented as local guidance or epidemiology.
- [ ] **Gaps or misleading wording:** I checked for important omissions or wording that could mislead users, including whether limits of this short summary are clear.
- [ ] **Scope:** The document gives no treatment instructions, medication doses, or unsupported Ethiopia-specific claims.
