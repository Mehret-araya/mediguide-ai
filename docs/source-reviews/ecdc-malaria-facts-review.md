# ECDC malaria facts: review summary

**Purpose:** Educational review for the question "What is malaria?"  
**Review date:** 2026-11-12  
**Source status:** Pending review; this summary does not approve or add the source to the knowledge corpus.

## General information

- **What it is:** Malaria is an acute or subacute infectious disease caused by *Plasmodium* parasites.
- **How it spreads:** It is mainly spread through the bite of an infected female *Anopheles* mosquito. ECDC also notes less common transmission through blood transfusion, organ transplantation, shared needles, and from a pregnant person to the fetus.
- **Common symptoms:** Fever and flu-like symptoms such as chills, headache, muscle aches, and feeling unwell.
- **Why it can be serious:** Malaria can be associated with anaemia and jaundice, and can cause kidney failure and an enlarged spleen. In cerebral malaria, infected red blood cells can clog small blood vessels in the brain; this can cause seizures, confusion, coma, and death.

## Ethiopia context
